Fjord Explorer (FT-01)
Where: Bergen, Flåm and the Nærøyfjord
Length: 4 days · Level: Easy · Max 16 travellers
Season: May to September
Dates: Weekly departures every Thursday: 13 May to 23 September 2027
Price: NOK 8,900 per person
A classic first trip to Norway. Ride the Flåm Railway down from the mountains, cruise the narrow Nærøyfjord between steep green walls, and spend a night in the village of Flåm. Calm, scenic and easy, with short walks only.

GOOD TO KNOW

Booking and deposit: A deposit of 20% of the trip price is due when you book. The rest is due 45 days before departure.
Cancellation by you: Cancel 30 days or more before departure: full refund minus a NOK 500 fee. Cancel 8 to 29 days before departure: 50% refund. Cancel 7 days or less before departure: no refund.
Cancellation by us: If we cancel a trip because of weather or too few travellers, you get a full refund or a free change to another date.
Children: Children under 12 get 25% off on all trips except the Trolltunga Challenge (minimum age 16) and the Svalbard Arctic Expedition (minimum age 10).
Groups: Groups of 6 or more travellers on the same booking get 10% off.
Prices: All prices are per person in Norwegian kroner (NOK), sharing a double room. Single room supplement: NOK 1,200 per trip.
Travel insurance: Travel insurance is not included. We strongly recommend it.
Accessibility: The Bergen City Break, Oslo Food and Culture and Norway in a Nutshell Classic are suitable for wheelchair users. Ask us about other trips.
